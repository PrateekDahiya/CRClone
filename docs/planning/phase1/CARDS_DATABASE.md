## STAT SCALING FORMULA

Clash Royale uses approximately 10% stat increase per level:
- Level 1 → Level 14: ~3.5x multiplier
- Tournament Standard = Level 11
- Level 11 stats shown above

Formula: `Stat_Lvl_N = Stat_Lvl_1 × (1.1)^(N-1)`

## ARENA LAYOUT

- **Size**: 18 tiles × 32 tiles (each side 18×16)
- **Bridge**: 2 tiles wide, center
- **River**: 4 tiles wide (impassable except flying)
- **Princess Towers**: 4 tiles from river, 3 tiles from side
- **King Tower**: Center back, 4 tiles from back
